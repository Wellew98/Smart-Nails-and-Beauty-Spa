 Nail & Spa as a luxury interactive beauty experience rather than a conventional salon website.

The core idea:

“Your nails are the main character.”

The site should feel elegant, tactile, feminine and premium. Motion should make the nail designs feel alive. It should never feel like a tech demo.

1. Overall visual direction

Style:

* Luxury beauty editorial
* Soft 3D photography
* Black, warm ivory, champagne and muted rose
* Large typography
* Lots of negative space
* Slow, smooth movement
* High-quality nail photography
* Glass effects used sparingly
* Subtle reflections and glow

Primary colours:

* Deep Espresso: #171313
* Warm Ivory: #F7F1EA
* Soft Rose: #D9BFC0
* Champagne: #D6B889
* Nude: #C9A995
* White: #FFFFFF

The dark sections create contrast. The ivory sections keep the site feeling clean.

2. Opening screen

The first screen should be spectacular.

Left side:

GRACE
NAIL & SPA

Then:

“Beautiful nails.
Beautifully done.”

Small text:

Nails, beauty and self-care in [location].

Below:

BOOK YOUR APPOINTMENT

Right side:

A large, photorealistic woman's hand with an exceptional nail set.

The hand is angled diagonally rather than sitting straight.

The fingers slowly move as if the hand is naturally floating.

The nails catch a soft light.

Every few seconds, the light travels across the nails.

This creates a small reflective shimmer.

3. Hero interaction

The cursor controls the lighting.

Move the mouse left:

The highlight moves across the nails.

Move right:

The hand subtly rotates.

Move up:

The camera moves slightly closer.

Move down:

The camera pulls back.

Keep the movement extremely subtle.

On mobile, replace cursor interaction with device-independent scroll movement.

The hand should not continuously spin like a product model. It should breathe.

4. Hero transition

When the visitor scrolls:

The hand moves toward the centre.

The background slowly darkens.

The words disappear.

The hand rotates approximately 20 degrees.

Then the hand moves toward the right side of the screen.

A new section appears underneath.

The effect should feel like one continuous scene rather than separate website sections.

20. Mobile design

Do not simply shrink the desktop version.

On mobile:

* Large hero hand
* Vertical nail selector
* Swipeable gallery
* Sticky booking button
* Large touch targets
* Reduced animation
* Optimized images
* No heavy 3D effects unless performance supports them

The hand interaction becomes swipe-based.

21. Technical architecture

I would build it with:

Next.js
React
Tailwind CSS
GSAP
Framer Motion
Three.js only where it adds real value
Supabase for booking/data
Cloudinary or optimized image hosting for large imagery

GSAP handles:

* Scroll animations
* Timeline animations
* Image transitions
* Parallax
* Hand movement

Three.js is optional.

I would not automatically use Three.js. A sequence of high-quality rendered images combined with GSAP could look better and load faster.

22. The important part: the hand

This is where I would spend most of the creative effort.

Instead of trying to create a generic 3D hand, generate a consistent photographic hand model.

Create approximately:

10 to 20 nail designs

3 to 5 hand positions

Different close-up angles

Then use those assets throughout the site.

For example:

Hand 01 + French
Hand 01 + Chrome
Hand 01 + Nude

Hand 02 + Acrylic
Hand 02 + Glam
Hand 02 + Art

The visitor starts recognising the hand and nail designs as part of the Grace brand.

24. Performance rules

This matters.

Do not build a spectacular site that loads slowly.

Use:

WebP/AVIF images
Lazy loading
Compressed hero assets
GPU-friendly transforms
Minimal blur effects
Minimal DOM animation
Reduced-motion support
Mobile-specific assets

The first screen needs to load quickly.

This is also a strong project for you because once you build the underlying system, you could turn the same approach into a reusable premium website system for other South African salons, spas, barbers, beauty clinics and similar businesses.
